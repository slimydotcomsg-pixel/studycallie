Welcome, Junior Scientist! 🔬

Guess what? Science isn't just memorizing big words — it's about being a super observant detective! You'll ask "Why?", group amazing living things, test cool materials, and run fair experiments just like a real scientist.

🦋 🧲 🌱
🔍

1. Observe & Classify

In P3, you will sort living things into groups (like Mammals vs Reptiles) by looking closely at their body coverings, how they breathe, and how they reproduce!

💬

2. Use Science Keywords

Teachers love clear Science words! Instead of "we can see through glass", say "allows most light to pass through".

⚖️

3. Play Fair in Experiments

A good scientist only changes ONE thing at a time! Everything else must stay the same so the test is fair. Try our Experiment Lab to practice!

🧪 Experiment Detective Lab

Every great scientist follows the Fair Test Rules! Learn the 3 secret variables below, then test your detective skills in our 2 interactive mini-games.

🔄

Changed Variable

Only ONE thing!

The 1 single thing you deliberately change to test.
Example: Type of material (Wood vs Plastic)

🔒

Unchanged Variables

Kept the SAME!

Everything else must stay identical so the test is fair!
Example: Same length, same thickness, same weights

📏

Measured Variable

The Result!

What you observe or measure at the end to get your answer!
Example: Number of weights before the ruler breaks
